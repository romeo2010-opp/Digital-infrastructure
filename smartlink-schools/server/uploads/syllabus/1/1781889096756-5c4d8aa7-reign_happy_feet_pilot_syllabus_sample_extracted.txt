3. Happy Feet Montessori Early Years - 4 Week Starter Plan
Level: Montessori Early Years / Preschool. Focus: gentle development, observation, practical activities, language
confidence, numeracy foundations, and parent visibility. No high-pressure exam mode should be used.
Week

Topic

Details for SmartLink extraction

1

Practical Life - Care of self
and environment

Subtopics: Hand washing, pouring, spooning, tidying learning space.
Objectives: Follow simple routines; improve coordination; show independence.
Skills: fine motor, independence, routine
Daily Drill: Optional 3-item picture routine check.
Assessment Notes: Teacher observation checklist.

1

Language - Listening and
oral expression

Subtopics: Name objects, follow two-step instructions, story circle.
Objectives: Listen attentively; answer simple questions; use new words.
Skills: listening, vocabulary, speaking
Daily Drill: Oral vocabulary game.
Assessment Notes: Anecdotal notes; no formal scoring.

2

Sensorial - Shapes, colours,
and matching

Subtopics: Sort by colour, match basic shapes, compare sizes.
Objectives: Identify common colours and shapes; group similar items.
Skills: classification, visual discrimination
Daily Drill: Picture matching activity.
Assessment Notes: Photo evidence or observation.

2

Mathematics - Counting 1-10

Subtopics: Count objects, match number symbols, compare more/less.
Objectives: Count accurately with objects; recognise numbers 1-10.
Skills: counting, one-to-one correspondence
Daily Drill: Counting objects 1-10.
Assessment Notes: Short activity check.

3

Culture - My school and
community

Subtopics: People at school, classroom roles, helping others.
Objectives: Identify school helpers; practise respectful behaviour.
Skills: social awareness, confidence
Daily Drill: Match helper to role.
Assessment Notes: Participation notes.

3

Language - Phonics
readiness

Subtopics: Initial sounds, rhymes, picture-word matching.
Objectives: Hear beginning sounds; match simple picture sounds.
Skills: phonemic awareness
Daily Drill: Initial sound picture drill.
Assessment Notes: Simple teacher-led oral check.

4

Practical Life - Grace and
courtesy

Subtopics: Greeting, sharing materials, waiting turn, saying thank you.
Objectives: Practise polite classroom behaviour and turn-taking.
Skills: social skills, self-regulation
Daily Drill: Scenario choice activity.
Assessment Notes: Observation summary for guardian.

4

Mathematics - Patterns and
sorting

Subtopics: AB patterns, object sorting by size/colour/type.
Objectives: Continue simple patterns; explain sorting rule.
Skills: patterning, reasoning
Daily Drill: Finish the pattern.
Assessment Notes: Practical task completion.

4. Cambridge Primary Stage 3 - English and Spelling
Level: Cambridge Primary-style Stage 3 starter plan. Subject: English / Spelling. Focus: reading fluency, vocabulary,
grammar, spelling patterns, and simple writing.
Week

Topic

Details for SmartLink extraction

1

Reading routines and
comprehension

Subtopics: Short stories, main idea, who/what/where questions.
Objectives: Read a short text and answer literal questions; identify main character and
setting.
Skills: reading, recall, comprehension
Daily Drill: 5 short comprehension questions.
Assessment Notes: Baseline reading check.

1

Spelling list 1

Subtopics: Short vowel words and common classroom words.
Objectives: Spell selected weekly words correctly and use them in oral sentences.
Skills: spelling, vocabulary
Daily Drill: 5-word spelling drill.
Assessment Notes: Friday spelling check.

2

Sentence building

Subtopics: Capital letters, full stops, simple sentence order.
Objectives: Write clear simple sentences using correct punctuation.
Skills: grammar, writing
Daily Drill: Fix-the-sentence questions.
Assessment Notes: Teacher marks short written responses.

2

Vocabulary growth

Subtopics: Synonyms, antonyms, word meaning from context.
Objectives: Use context to understand common words; choose suitable alternatives.
Skills: vocabulary, reasoning
Daily Drill: Multiple choice word meaning.
Assessment Notes: Low-stakes class quiz.

3

Grammar basics

Subtopics: Nouns, verbs, adjectives.
Objectives: Identify nouns, verbs, and adjectives in simple sentences.
Skills: grammar, classification
Daily Drill: Label word type in sentences.
Assessment Notes: Auto-mark objective items.

3

Spelling list 2

Subtopics: Consonant blends and common high-frequency words.
Objectives: Spell weekly words and recognise patterns.
Skills: spelling, phonics
Daily Drill: Spelling + sentence completion.
Assessment Notes: Friday spelling check.

4

Writing short paragraphs

Subtopics: Topic sentence, supporting details, neat ending.
Objectives: Write a short paragraph about school/home using 3-5 sentences.
Skills: composition, organisation
Daily Drill: Choose best sentence order.
Assessment Notes: Teacher review for writing.

6. Cambridge Primary Stage 3 - Mathematics
Subject: Mathematics. Focus: number confidence, operations, measurement, shapes, and problem solving.
Week

Topic

Details for SmartLink extraction

1

Number and place value

Subtopics: Counting, ordering, tens and ones, compare numbers.
Objectives: Read, write, order, and compare numbers within the class range.
Skills: number sense, comparison
Daily Drill: 8 number questions.
Assessment Notes: Baseline number check.

1

Addition and subtraction
review

Subtopics: Mental addition, subtraction, missing numbers.
Objectives: Add and subtract simple numbers using mental or written strategies.
Skills: calculation, fluency
Daily Drill: Mixed addition/subtraction.
Assessment Notes: Auto-mark objective items.

2

Multiplication foundations

Subtopics: Repeated addition, 2x, 5x, 10x facts.
Objectives: Represent multiplication as equal groups and repeated addition.
Skills: multiplication, reasoning
Daily Drill: Times-table fluency.
Assessment Notes: Teacher checks working.

2

Word problems

Subtopics: One-step problems using addition/subtraction.
Objectives: Choose the correct operation for a simple word problem.
Skills: problem-solving
Daily Drill: Story problem daily drill.
Assessment Notes: Track wrong-operation mistakes.

3

Measurement

Subtopics: Length, mass, capacity, choosing units.
Objectives: Use appropriate units and compare measurements.
Skills: measurement, estimation
Daily Drill: Unit choice and comparison.
Assessment Notes: Practical measurement task.

3

Geometry

Subtopics: 2D shapes, sides, corners, symmetry intro.
Objectives: Identify common shapes and describe simple properties.
Skills: geometry, classification
Daily Drill: Shape property questions.
Assessment Notes: Auto-mark MCQ.

4

Fractions introduction

Subtopics: Halves, quarters, equal parts, simple diagrams.
Objectives: Recognise halves and quarters as equal parts of a whole.
Skills: fractions, visual reasoning
Daily Drill: Picture-based fractions.
Assessment Notes: Teacher review for diagrams.

7. Sample Mathematics Question Seeds
Topic

Question / Answer

Explanation

Place Value

Type: multiple_choice
Question: What is the value of 5 in 352? A. 5 B. 50 C. 500
Correct answer: B

In 352, the 5 is in the tens place, so its value is
50.

Addition

Type: short_answer
Question: 27 + 15 = ?
Correct answer: 42

Add ones first, then tens. 27 + 15 = 42.

Fractions

Type: multiple_choice
Question: Which fraction means one out of two equal
parts? A. 1/4 B. 1/2 C. 2/3
Correct answer: B

One out of two equal parts is one half, written
as 1/2.

8. Cambridge Primary Stage 3 - Science
Subject: Science. Focus: observation, questioning, living things, materials, forces, and simple scientific enquiry.
Week

Topic

Details for SmartLink extraction

1

Living things

Subtopics: Plants and animals, basic needs, habitats.
Objectives: Describe what living things need and identify simple habitats.
Skills: observation, classification
Daily Drill: Living/non-living questions.
Assessment Notes: Class discussion + quiz.

1

Scientific enquiry

Subtopics: Observe, predict, record.
Objectives: Make simple observations and record findings using words or pictures.
Skills: enquiry, recording
Daily Drill: Observation vocabulary.
Assessment Notes: Practical observation task.

2

Plants

Subtopics: Parts of a plant, roots, stem, leaves, flowers.
Objectives: Name common parts of a plant and describe simple functions.
Skills: recall, explanation
Daily Drill: Plant part matching.
Assessment Notes: Auto-mark matching/MCQ.

2

Materials

Subtopics: Wood, plastic, metal, glass, fabric.
Objectives: Identify materials and describe properties such as hard, soft, smooth, rough.
Skills: classification, vocabulary
Daily Drill: Material property questions.
Assessment Notes: Practical sorting activity.

3

Forces and movement

Subtopics: Push, pull, movement, simple speed/direction changes.
Objectives: Describe pushes and pulls and observe how objects move.
Skills: cause-effect, observation
Daily Drill: Push/pull examples.
Assessment Notes: Teacher-led practical notes.

3

Health and hygiene

Subtopics: Clean hands, food, exercise, rest.
Objectives: Explain basic habits that keep the body healthy.
Skills: health literacy
Daily Drill: Short scenario questions.
Assessment Notes: Parent summary note.

4

Earth and weather

Subtopics: Weather observation, clouds, rain, sun, wind.
Objectives: Observe daily weather and use simple weather words.
Skills: observation, recording
Daily Drill: Weather vocabulary.
Assessment Notes: Mini weather diary.
